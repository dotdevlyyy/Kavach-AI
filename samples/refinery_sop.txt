MRPL Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Unit: Crude Distillation Unit (CDU)
Subject: Corrosion Monitoring and Mitigation

1. Objective
To monitor and mitigate corrosion in the CDU overhead system and distillation towers.

2. Responsibilities
- Panel Operator: Monitor overhead temperature and pH levels.
- Field Operator: Collect water samples and check chemical injection pumps.
- Inspection Engineer: Conduct routine ultrasonic thickness (UT) measurements.

3. Procedure
3.1. Wash Water Injection
- Ensure wash water injection rate is maintained at 5-7% of crude feed rate.
- If chloride levels in the sour water exceed 50 ppm, increase wash water injection by 10%.

3.2. Neutralizing Amine Injection
- Monitor the pH of the overhead accumulator boot water. Target pH is 5.5 to 6.5.
- Adjust amine injection pump stroke to maintain target pH.

3.3. Filming Amine Injection
- Maintain continuous dosage of filming amine as per vendor recommendations.

4. Emergency Response
- If overhead pH drops below 4.0, immediately notify the shift supervisor and increase neutralizing amine dosage to maximum safe capacity.
